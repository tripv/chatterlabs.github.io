🎬 Spot the Shams

A line-guessing game — in two rounds.

We're studying whether AI can believeably replicate the voice and tone of a TV show like Seinfeld. On the following slides, you will be shown real Seinfeld scenes, followed by lines that are either real, or written by AI.

Round 1: Continuation: You will read a scene and be asked to pick the next line the real writers wrote. The other choices were written by AI.

Round 2: Infilling: You'll see the lines before and after a masked line, and be asked to pick the real line that belongs in the middle. One choice is real, the other is written by AI.

This quiz is anonymous and takes about 5 minutes. 20 questions in total.

Round 2 — fill the gap

You'll see the lines before and after a masked line (the dashed box). Pick the line the real writers actually put there.

Hint: pick the line that actually connects to what comes next, not just sound right.
